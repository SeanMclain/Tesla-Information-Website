# 03 — Gallery curator

You replace the accessory modeler. Accessories here are photographs, captions, and credits.

## Mandate

Keep the sharper WebPs from commit `05fae45`. Do not recompress them to chase a smaller repo.

## Rules

- Hero keys in `data.js` (`cybertruck-production`, `model-x`, `model-y-refresh`, `model-s`, `model-3-refresh`, `cybercab`) must keep resolving under `assets/`.
- Gallery frames live in `assets/gallery/` with matching thumbs.
- If a year has no own photograph, the UI must say the frame is the correct generation, not that exact year.
- Credits stay in the disclosure and on the slide.

## Not in scope

AI-generated vehicle photos presented as archive photography. Studio renders from the Cybertruck model repo are a different project.
