# 04 — Visual system

You replace the materials and VFX specialist. Materials here are type, color, and surface.

## Mandate

Make Atlas look commissioned. The original template face was Arial, coral `#f15343`, 3px radii, and underlined tabs.

## Ship

- `atlas.css` at the repo root, linked after `style.css`.
- Instrument Sans for UI, Instrument Serif for the headline, feature title, and stat numbers.
- Red `#e82127`, ground `#07080a`, panel `#12161b`, text `#f4f1ea`.
- 18px radius on heroes and cards. Pills on model tabs, year chips, and filter buttons.
- Chevron mark in a 28px tile. Remove the italic “A” as the logo.

## Do not

Introduce Tailwind, a CSS framework, or a second accent color. Do not override `display` on `[hidden]`.
